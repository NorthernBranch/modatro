Inside Modatro

Your mod collection, in one place.

Browse Balatro mods from Thunderstore or an optional mod index. Check Lovely and Steamodded requirements, install supported dependencies, and keep a record of your files.

Your managed selections

A profile for each way you play.

Create named selections, review dependencies, and switch safely. Export a profile to back it up or share it; import checks the file and installs required mods and prerequisites. Mod versions and configurations stay shared.

Help with your setup

Understand what needs attention.

See enabled and disabled mods, setup checks, and recent changes in Troubleshooting. Preview and edit a support report, with log excerpts included only when you choose them.

Screenshots use representative selections and synthetic diagnostics.

Experimental and optional

Start with a small mod of your own.

Build a Joker, Deck or Voucher with dedicated controls, or write your own Lua. Configure scoring, starting cards or redemption effects, choose original built-in artwork or upload your own, then export a mod ZIP. Enable it in Settings; drafts stay separate from installed mods and everything is created locally.

A little preparation

Keep a copy of your saves.

Mods can cause crashes or affect your saves. Back up your Balatro saves before installing or updating mods. Modatro’s file-operation backups protect recorded mod changes; they do not back up your game saves.

Find backup guidance in Settings and a dismissible reminder in the app. Mod Creator is off by default; generated and custom mods still need testing in-game.
